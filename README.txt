KINGDOMS OF CHAOS · SOCIAL LIFE & COMMERCE UPDATE (v4.1)
========================================================

HOW TO PLAY
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a current version of Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Click Begin Your Reign. Click once in the page to enable the soundtrack.

WHAT'S NEW IN v4.1 (includes all v4.0 worldbuilding features)
- Expanded map: 144 × 144 tiles (65% more world area than the previous 112 × 112 map).
- Six biomes: Tundra, Forest, Grassland, Desert, Wetlands and Highlands, with climate-driven terrain palettes and different tree density.
- Individual civilian genes: citizens have varied skin tones, hair colours, hair styles, clothes, trim and patterns. Children inherit a mix of both parents' appearance genes, with small chances of variation; family names and parent information are also carried forward.
- Blueprint construction: place a building plan and a Builder walks over to construct it. Blueprints show their progress. Train Builders at the Castle with Y.
- Greenhouse: unlocked in the Medieval Age; plants a new tree on open land about every 12 seconds, helping restore wood supply.
- Four milestone-based ages:
    Stone Age — huts, farms, mines, campfires, clubs and spears.
    Medieval Age — swords, armour, watchtowers and greenhouses.
    Production Era — factories, factory workers, automated supplies and guns.
    Modern Age — research labs, advanced combat visuals and energy weapons.
  Advancing requires the listed population and building milestones. Each age adds a resource bonus, increases production and expands build range.
- Factory Workers improve the automated supply production of factories. Research Labs produce gold over time.
- Redesigned title screen, in-game interface, building menu, age progress panel and building visuals.
- Civilian social life: idle adult workers and soldiers can approach other citizens in their own kingdom and have conversations. Dialogue varies between friendly, everyday and tense exchanges, with relationship scores that can change over repeated conversations.
- Different relationships: citizens may be strangers, acquaintances, friends, best friends, partners, family or rivals. Relationship history appears when you inspect a civilian; close relationships and families can form over time.
- Village Talk panel: read the latest conversations in the HUD. Click “Focus / read” on a conversation to zoom the camera toward those citizens. At close zoom, short speech bubbles appear above speakers while the panel keeps the full dialogue text readable.
- Era-based commerce: the Medieval Age unlocks the Merchant Market, the Production Era unlocks the General Store, and the Modern Age unlocks the Supermarket. Shopkeepers walk to their stores, visit the frontage and generate trade income; later stores produce both gold and food.
- Commerce blueprints: when a kingdom reaches a new era and has the needed resources, it can queue the era’s shop as a blueprint. Builders must finish the construction, and adult civilians are assigned as merchants. You can also select a shop blueprint yourself.

CONTROLS
- Drag: select citizens. Click: inspect units, buildings and resources.
- Right-click: move, attack or gather.
- WASD: pan camera. Mouse wheel: zoom. H: return to your castle.
- 1–0: select the first ten building blueprints. C: Merchant Market. V: General Store. U: Supermarket. Esc: cancel placement.
- Y: train Builder. Q: train Worker. E: train Soldier. X: train Factory Worker.
- R: Fishing Boat. T: Warship. P: pause. M: music. N: sound effects. G: auto-work.

ECONOMY NOTES
- Workers automatically gather wood and stone when auto-work is enabled.
- Farms produce food, Mines produce gold and citizens consume food over time. Keep food production positive.
- Houses increase the population cap and families may have children when there is enough food and room.
- Blueprints reserve their plot and consume resources when placed; an available Builder is needed to finish them. Era-based shops use the same construction process.
- The Greenhouse only plants on suitable open grassland within its nearby growth area.

AUDIO
The game includes three music tracks in audio/ plus synthesised sound effects. If sound does not begin immediately, click anywhere once and check the in-game volume controls.
